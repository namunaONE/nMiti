## [Unreleased] — R10.5 rung D: Delta-T horizon term (HYA-445)

- New public surface `deltaTHorizonAtJdUt(jdUt, nowJdUt)` — the one Delta-T error-bar function: the honest uncertainty of the Delta-T behind any served value, given the day the caller serves. The channel and every served value are untouched; the engine never reads a clock (the caller states today, so receipts are reproducible). Wall-clock mapping stated per the rung B frame audit: 1 s Delta-T = 1 s boundary time on the TT channels, zero on the UT-direct (sunrise/sunset/moonrise) channels.
- The composition is measured, never invented: (1) the USNO error column is an estimate, not a bound — over the vendored overlap (15 shadowed prediction rows, 2022-07 .. 2026-01) realized |prediction − observed| exceeded the stated error by up to 2.31× (worst: 2023-10, 0.062 s vs 0.027 s stated; independent hyper-calendar vendoring of the same file measured up to 2.7×), so the preds-era envelope is the stated error × `deltaTPredsOverlapInflationFactor` (recomputed from the tables at every evaluation); (2) the declared +8.91 s EM join step is a level systematic of the *served* polynomial — R7a's 1 s knot at the join covered the USNO trajectory's own error, not the polynomial sitting the full step above it — so the EM-era envelope composes `deltaTEmJoinDisagreementSeconds` in quadrature with the unchanged knots (1/6/12/20 s at 2033.75/2050/2100/2150): ~8.96 s honest at the join, 21.8 s by 2149; the knots stand corroborated by the vendored SMH2021 future error table (2 s at 2030, 6 s at 2050, 10 s at 2100) and Espenak's σ = 0.8·t².
- History never ages: dates inside the observations keep the R7a declaration (0.05 s; the published SMH2021 table before 1973-02) whatever today is. `deltaTObservationsFreshnessWindowDays` (497 d, the canary's ~16-month guarantee) plus the per-call `observationsCurrent` verdict state whether the tables were still fresh relative to the caller's today.
- New permanent CI gate `test/delta_t_horizon_test.dart`: the measured constants, exact per-segment composition (never below the R7a declaration), strict growth across the forecast horizon, matured/forecast classification, the freshness window, determinism, argument range. No served-value, fixture, band, receipt or receipt-corpus changes; the display layer (today.json honesty fields, the honesty pages) consumes this surface in a later arc. The one build-pipeline change is the `_mdToHtml` hardening bullet below.
- Site build hardening, found while landing this rung: a markdown line starting with `|` that is NOT a table (no separator row follows) used to hang `_mdToHtml` in `build_site.dart` forever — the paragraph branch broke on the pipe without consuming the line, so the outer loop re-read it forever, appending empty paragraphs until the CI runner was OOM-killed ~22–30 min in (GitHub shows the kill as "The operation is canceled", not a real cancel; six CI runs were lost to it, isolated by two diagnostic probe PRs — an inert-file probe green in 1m57s, a docs-only probe hanging). The renderer now always advances — a lone pipe line renders as its own paragraph — so the class of hang cannot recur. The trigger was one hard-wrapped line of this rung docs beginning with the literal `|date − today|`; it was re-wrapped, text unchanged. No served values, fixtures, bands or receipts changed; docs.html output is byte-identical for every well-formed table.
- The site builder `packages/nmiti_cli/bin/build_site.dart` is now a 30-line library (the imports, the shared month and weekday name constants, six `part` directives) over six page-family part files — json, docs, md, astro, festivals and masa, each at most ~31 KB, the renderer hardening living in `build_site_md.dart`. A mechanical, content-neutral split: every part is a byte-identical slice of the previous single file plus the fix, verified by fetch-back comparison; no served output, contract marker or checksum changes.

## [Unreleased] — R10.5 rung C: solver convergence cross-check (HYA-445)

- The wrap-safe limb boundary solver now solves every crossing twice and throws on disagreement: the served 60-iteration bisection upper bound (numerics unchanged, output bit-identical) plus an independent derivative-accelerated Newton solve from the same coarse 0.25 d bracket — wrap-safe signed distance to the boundary angle, central-difference rate (step 1e-4 d), bracket-clamped steps, quadratic convergence to machine precision in ~3–4 iterations (~15 angle evaluations vs the bisection's ~63). Agreement gate 1e-8 d (~0.86 ms, ~9× the bisection's double-precision residue); StateError otherwise — fail closed.
- Convergence documented in docs/methodology.md: the bisection is bit-converged after ~35 of its declared 60 iterations (one ULP at JD 2.46e6 ≈ 47 µs), the served value sits within ~2 ULP (~100 µs) of the true crossing, and the Newton path deliberately starts from the coarse bracket so agreement is a genuine two-method result, not a clamp artifact.
- New permanent CI gate `packages/nepali_panchang/test/solver_convergence_test.dart`: full sector-chain sweeps (every tithi sector crossed twice, every karana/nakshatra/yoga sector at least once) plus seeded-random windows over AD 1975–2100, asserting Newton-vs-bisection agreement on every crossing and the sector index flipping across it. ## [Unreleased] — R10.5 rung B: frame audit — consistent separation and sidereal frames (HYA-445)

- The rung B audit walked every solver path's reference frame (ΔT→TT on input, nutation and light-time on output) and found one real frame inconsistency: the tithi/karana elongation subtracted the nutated apparent Sun (`sunApparentLongitude`) from the geometric moon (Meeus Ch.47) — a frame rotation applied to one body of a separation — so every tithi/karana angle carried −Δψ (±17.2″ over the 18.6-year node cycle; +8.0″ in the corpus window ≈ 16 s of boundary time). The same class sat in the sidereal paths: nakshatra used a mean-equinox moon where the publishers' convention is apparent-sidereal, and yoga nutated the Sun side only.
- Fix: new `sunLongitudeForSeparationDeg` in sun.dart — true longitude + aberration, nutation omitted (a common rotation cancels in a separation; each body's light-time stays) — now feeds the tithi/karana elongation, deduplicating the exact frame the solar eclipse solver always used privately; nakshatra and yoga apply the nutation to the moon (`nutationInLongitudeDeg`, factored out of `sunApparentLongitude`), putting every body in the publishers' apparent-sidereal convention.
- Named, deliberately skipped contributor: the Moon's own light-time (~1.3 s of time, ~0.7″ ≈ 1.4 s of boundary time) — computing it would double the moon evaluations inside every bisection for a term far below the publisher minute floor.
- Every served boundary shifts ~14–16 s earlier in the window (ν = +8.0″: tithi/karana ~16 s, nakshatra ~15 s, yoga ~14 s). All corpus gates re-verify in CI on this change — measured, not asserted: the receipt corpora bands, the bounded karana knife-edge rule, the NPNS 730-day audit floor, the festival gates, and the rung A property suite. The fitted per-publisher ayanamsa profiles (BhaktiBharat −0.0795°, Hamro Patro −0.0117°) are NOT re-fit here — deferred to the next receipt-driven calibration crawl so a convention change and a fit change never land in one PR.
- The full per-path audit table (sunrise/sunset ΔT-free by construction; moonrise UT→TT inside; masa and both eclipse paths convert bounds; lunar eclipse geometric on both bodies; solar true+aberration with the shared helper) is in docs/methodology.md. The Swiss-truth tithi/karana signed mean (−14.3 s post-R7a, previously attributed to the Meeus Ch.47 truncation alone) was mostly this nutation asymmetry — measured on this PR's CI, all bands PASS: tithi 4.0 s mean / 11 s max / +1.9 s signed (n=47), karana 4.1 / 11 / +1.9 (n=93), nakshatra 66.2 → 51.2 s and yoga 110.8 → 96.8 s (the moon now in the publishers' apparent-sidereal frame, so the remaining sidereal gap is the ayanamsa profile alone), sunrise / moonrise / eclipse channels and the ΔT residual unmoved. The uncertainty basis strings and the methodology Swiss-truth section now carry the post-fix scoreboard.

The ΔT source gap (our Espenak–Meeus 75.6 s vs observed ≈69 s for 2026) is confirmed from three independent directions: the corrected Swiss residual (mean −6.79 s, max 6.81 s, n=233 → Swiss internal ΔT ≈68.8 s), the USNO/IERS long-term predictions table (69.05 ± 0.19 s at 2026.0), and community practice (e.g. AstronomyKit’s default switch to interpolated IERS values).
- Decision: keep the Espenak–Meeus polynomial — deterministic and maintenance-free; the ~6.6 s systematic is sub-band (all 9 Swiss-truth bands pass with 4–15× headroom) and below the publisher minute floor. A ΔT-source swap would shift every boundary time ~6.6 s and force a corpus re-baseline for no measurable gain.
- Adjudication text added to `uncertainty.dart` (named-contributors doc block; header corrected to three anchors) and `docs/methodology.md` (Swiss-truth section). No engine, data, band, or site changes.
